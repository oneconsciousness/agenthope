The ten skills

Hope is ten skills. You set up once, show your work, then run the hunt a little every day. Each skill is a plain-language set of instructions: open one to read it, or point the AI you already use straight at it.

One promise holds across all ten: Hope never invents a fact about you — every line it writes traces back to your own work and your own words.

Start

Start

The front door. Hope reads your hunt folder, tells you where you stand, and points you to the next step.

 Step 1

Onboarding

Tell Hope your story once. It builds your career file, the answers forms always ask, and your hunt folder.

 Step 2

Portfolio & résumé

Hope's signature skill. A designed page recruiters stop for, with a living timeline of your career, and one clean résumé.

 Step 3

Publish

Put it on the web — one shareable link you own. Hope carries the whole technical load for you.

 Step 4

Find jobs

Hope sweeps job boards, reads the whole description, and screens against your rules. You get a shortlist. You pick.

 Step 5

Apply

Give it a job link. Hope fills the form in your browser, writes down every answer, and stops at submit. You press send.

 Step 6

Write

Emails, LinkedIn messages, cover letters and follow-ups in your own voice. It learns from every edit. You send.

 Step 7

Interview

A reminder to record the call, a prep file before it, and a debrief from your recording after.

 Step 8

Sync

Each morning Hope reads your email and LinkedIn, read-only, updates your tracker, and tells you what needs you.

 Step 9

Tracker

One spreadsheet you own. Every role, its status, and the next action. Ask where you stand any time.

What changed in 2.0

Hope now follows how a real job hunt runs, start to finish. Three skills retired: Skill Gap, Proof Projects and the Dashboard. In a real four-month hunt they never got used as separate steps. Their best idea, an honest read of how well you fit each role, now comes with every role in Find jobs and Apply. The tracker took the dashboard's place. Looking for one of them? The skill you want is most likely Find jobs, Apply or Tracker.
