What you get

The whole hunt, all yours.

◆

A portfolio that gets read

A designed page with a living timeline of your career visitors can play, hover, and click. Not a form, not a template — a page that looks like you tried.

▤

A résumé (CV) the screeners read cleanly

Key phrases bolded for the 7-second skim, links clickable, text never too small. The screening software parses it cleanly, and a person actually wants to read it.

↗

One link you own

Published free to a page in your name. Drop it on LinkedIn and it unfurls with your own preview card. Only you can change it.

▦

Real openings that fit your rules

Say “find me jobs” and Hope sweeps job boards through their own data feeds, reads each full description, and screens out what breaks your rules: your pay floor, titles you don't want, too many live applications at one company. You get a shortlist. You pick.

✓

Applications filled. You press send.

Give Hope a job link. It saves the posting, fills the form in your browser while you watch, and writes down every answer it gave and every one it left for you. Then it stops at submit. Sending is yours.

✎

Every message in your voice

Emails, LinkedIn messages, cover letters, follow-ups, replies to recruiters. Drafted the way you write and saved for you to send. Every edit you make teaches Hope a little more about how you write.

◉

Ready for every interview

The moment a call is booked, a reminder to record it, with your prep inside. Before: what to land, your honest gaps, two questions to ask. After: a debrief from your recording and a follow-up to send.

↻

Each morning, what changed

Hope reads your email and LinkedIn, only reads, and updates your tracker with confirmations, rejections and recruiter messages. Then it tells you, in a few lines, what needs you today.

▥

One tracker you own

Every role, its status, the next action and dated notes, in one spreadsheet in your folder. Ask “where do I stand?” any time. Open it, sort it, edit it. It's yours.
